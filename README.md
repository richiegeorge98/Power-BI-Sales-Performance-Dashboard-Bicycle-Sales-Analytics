Power BI Sales Performance Dashboard | Bicycle Sales Analytics 
📊 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing bicycle and bicycle-related product sales across different countries from 2015 to 2017 using Microsoft Power BI. The interactive Sales Overview dashboard provides insights into sales performance, order volume, product subcategories, customer segments, geographic distribution, and product returns.

The objective is to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights through data cleaning, KPI development, data visualization, and exploratory data analysis. The dashboard helps stakeholders identify sales trends, evaluate product performance, monitor return rates, and uncover opportunities to improve revenue and customer satisfaction.

🎯 Business Objectives
Analyze overall sales performance across 2015, 2016, and 2017.
Monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), including total sales, order count, total returns, and return percentage.
Identify top-performing product subcategories, customer segments, and countries.
Evaluate monthly sales trends and compare actual performance against targets.
Analyze product returns to identify opportunities for operational improvement.
Support data-driven decision-making through interactive dashboards and visual analytics.
🛠️ Tools & Technologies

DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – KPI calculations and analytical measures
Data Visualization – KPI cards, bar charts, time-series charts, maps, and interactive filters

📌 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard tracks the following business metrics:

Total Sales: Overall revenue generated from bicycle and related product sales.
Order Count: Total number of orders recorded in the dataset.
Total Returns: Number of returned orders or return records, based on the dashboard's measure definition.
Return Rate (%): Percentage of orders returned relative to the total order count.
⚙️ Project Workflow
1. Data Collection

Imported sales data covering 2015–2017, including product categories, customer information, order details, dates, countries, and return-related information.

2. Data Cleaning & Transformation
Handled missing, invalid, and inconsistent values.
Standardized date formats and relevant data fields.
Prepared and transformed datasets for analysis and visualization.
Organized product, customer, sales, and return information for reporting.
3. Data Modeling & KPI Development
Developed analytical measures to calculate sales, order counts, returns, and return rates.
Organized data dimensions to support analysis by year, country, product category, customer, and demographic segment.
Incorporated interactive filtering and parameter-based price adjustments to explore changes in sales values.
4. Dashboard Development

Created interactive visualizations to analyze:

Total sales by product subcategory and customer segment.
Sales and adjusted sales trends over time.
Yearly sales performance across countries.
Sales distribution by parent status and customer demographics.
Geographic sales distribution using map visualizations.
Customer-level sales contributions.
5. Insights & Business Recommendations

Interpreted dashboard results to identify high-performing product categories, geographic sales patterns, return-rate concerns, and opportunities to improve sales performance.

📈 Key Insights
Overall Performance
Total Sales: $24.91 million.
Total Orders: 25,160.
Total Returns: 1,828.
Return Rate: 7.26%.
Product Performance
Road bikes generated approximately $8 million in sales in the high-income segment.
Road bikes and mountain bikes each generated approximately $2 million in the medium-income segment.
Mountain bikes led the low-income segment with approximately $5 million in sales, followed by touring bikes at approximately $2 million.
Other subcategories, including tires, helmets, and jerseys, contributed comparatively lower sales.
Sales Trends & Target Analysis
Sales trends fluctuated over time, with notable increases during certain months, including March and April 2017.
In June 2017, sales reached approximately $1.83 million against a target of $1.77 million, exceeding the target by 3.31%.
June 2017 order volume reached 2,146 against a target of 2,165, falling short by approximately 0.88%.
An interactive price adjustment parameter enabled users to explore adjusted sales scenarios.
Geographic & Customer Analysis
Sales activity was concentrated in North America, Europe, and parts of Asia.
Customer-level analysis enabled comparisons of individual contributions to total sales.
The dashboard highlighted customer-level sales exceeding $4,000, supporting further investigation of high-value customer behavior.
💡 Business Recommendations
Improve Return Management: Investigate product quality, customer expectations, and order fulfillment processes to identify potential causes of returns.
Optimize Product Strategy: Maintain focus on strong-performing road and mountain bike categories while evaluating growth opportunities in lower-performing subcategories.
Strengthen Regional Sales Strategies: Use country-level sales insights to identify high-performing markets and regions with untapped potential.
Improve Order Conversion: Investigate why order volume fell slightly below the June 2017 target and develop strategies to improve order performance.
Enhance Customer Segmentation: Use customer-level sales patterns to support targeted marketing, customer retention, and personalized promotional campaigns.
Monitor Sales Performance: Track monthly KPIs and sales trends to evaluate progress against business targets.
